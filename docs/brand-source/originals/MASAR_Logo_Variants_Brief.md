# MASAR Logo System --- Variant Generation Brief

**Brand:** MASAR / مسار\
**Document:** Logo variant and production specification\
**Version:** 1.0\
**Status:** Creative direction approved; final vector master and
production measurements still require approval.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Purpose

This brief contains the information needed to create a consistent family
of MASAR logo variants from one approved master logo.

It is intended for a graphic designer, brand designer, motion designer,
or image-generation workflow. The goal is not to invent a new identity
for every application. Every variant must remain recognizably part of
the same MASAR identity.

The approved direction is:

-   Arabic-first identity: **مسار**
-   A distinctive, tall, elongated Arabic calligraphic wordmark
-   Refined, narrow vertical proportions and smooth continuous curves
-   Minimal English typography: **MASAR**
-   A premium, restrained, monochrome foundation
-   Deep green and mint as supporting brand colors, used with restraint
-   No generic bus, road, location-pin, or arrow icon as a replacement
    for the wordmark

**Core principle:** Create the master logo once, then derive all
variants from it. Do not redraw the lettering differently for each
format.

## 2. Brand and visual character

### 2.1 Meaning

MASAR (مسار) means route, path, course, or trajectory. The identity
should suggest direction, continuity, connection, and confident movement
without relying on literal transportation symbols.

### 2.2 Personality

  Attribute   Visual expression
  ----------- ---------------------------------------------------------
  Premium     Precise curves, careful spacing, restrained composition
  Modern      Minimal forms and clean supporting typography
  Confident   Strong silhouette and deliberate proportions
  Elegant     Tall Arabic letterforms and controlled negative space
  Clear       Legible Arabic spelling and a simple hierarchy
  Connected   Consistent construction across every variant

### 2.3 Relationship to MALAKY Studio

MASAR is its own product identity, developed within the broader MALAKY
Studio design world. It may share the refined, elongated Arabic
calligraphic sensibility and monochrome restraint, but it must have its
own original and correctly written **مسار** wordmark.

Do not copy the MALAKY lettering or use the MALAKY logo as the MASAR
mark.

## 3. Master logo construction

The master artwork is the source of truth for every subsequent variant.

### 3.1 Primary wordmark

-   Arabic word: **مسار**
-   English support text: **MASAR**
-   Arabic is the hero; English is secondary.
-   Use the approved custom Arabic calligraphy, not a generic font
    substitution.
-   Keep the Arabic mark vertically expressive, with elongated upright
    strokes and controlled rounded terminals.
-   Preserve the exact approved letter shapes, connections, dots,
    proportions, and spacing.
-   English lettering should be a restrained, modern sans-serif with
    generous tracking.
-   Keep the English line optically centered under the Arabic mark in
    the stacked version.

### 3.2 Arabic correctness

The Arabic must read exactly **مسار** (meem, seen, alif, raa).

The final vector master must be checked by a fluent Arabic reader or
Arabic type specialist. Confirm:

-   Correct spelling and letter order
-   Correct connections and joins
-   Correct placement and shape of dots
-   Correct direction of reading
-   No accidental extra strokes that suggest another letter
-   Legibility at small sizes

A decorative result that does not read correctly is not an acceptable
logo.

### 3.3 Geometry

Do not invent final numerical proportions before the master artwork is
approved. The designer should:

1.  Refine the Arabic mark as vector paths.
2.  Establish a consistent stroke-weight logic.
3.  Balance the tallest strokes against the lower letterforms.
4.  Check internal counters and negative spaces.
5.  Set the English line's width and tracking optically.
6.  Record the final proportions in the brand guideline.
7.  Use the same master paths in all variants.

## 4. Required logo variants

Create the following variants from the approved master.

### Variant A --- Primary stacked bilingual logo

**Purpose:** Main brand signature for website headers, presentations,
marketing, and formal brand use.

Construction: - Arabic **مسار** centered above. - English **MASAR**
centered below. - Arabic mark is visually dominant. - English line is
smaller, widely tracked, and understated. - Maintain generous clear
space around the full lockup.

Preferred treatments: - White logo on black or deep-green background. -
Black logo on white or light-neutral background.

### Variant B --- Arabic-only logo

**Purpose:** Arabic-first applications, social media, local marketing,
and compact brand placements.

Construction: - Use the exact Arabic master paths. - Do not add English
text. - Preserve the same proportions and silhouette as the primary
logo. - Center the mark optically within its canvas.

### Variant C --- English-only wordmark

**Purpose:** Applications where Arabic is not practical or where a
compact Latin wordmark is required.

Construction: - Text: **MASAR** - Use the approved Latin typeface or
custom lettering. - Use considered letter spacing; avoid excessive
tracking. - Keep the style understated and consistent with the Arabic
master. - Do not create an unrelated symbol or type treatment.

### Variant D --- Horizontal bilingual lockup

**Purpose:** Desktop navigation bars, document headers, partner
listings, and narrow horizontal placements.

Construction: - Arabic mark and English wordmark arranged side by
side. - Arabic remains the dominant element. - English should be aligned
optically to the Arabic mark, not mechanically to its bounding box. -
Maintain enough space between the two elements to avoid a cramped
appearance. - Create both RTL-appropriate and LTR-appropriate placement
rules if the product requires both.

### Variant E --- Compact brand mark

**Purpose:** App icon, favicon, avatar, and very small placements.

Construction: - Explore a simplified crop or compact monogram derived
from the Arabic master. - It must be based on actual approved letterform
geometry. - Do not introduce a generic transport icon. - Do not squeeze
the full bilingual lockup into a tiny square. - Test recognizability at
16, 24, 32, 48, and 64 px. - If the Arabic-derived compact mark is not
legible at small sizes, use a carefully designed typographic fallback
rather than forcing it.

**Approval gate:** The compact mark is not final until it is
recognizable without the full wordmark and does not create an incorrect
Arabic reading.

### Variant F --- App icon artwork

**Purpose:** iOS, Android, and app-store presentation.

Create: - Primary app icon on deep green or black. - Light-background
alternative if needed. - A simplified, centered mark with generous
internal padding. - A version tested against rounded-square masks and
platform icon crops.

Rules: - Do not put tiny supporting text inside the icon. - Keep the
silhouette readable at small sizes. - Do not add ornamental details that
disappear when scaled down. - Supply the mark as artwork with a
safe-area guide; platform-specific export sizes can be generated later.

### Variant G --- Favicon and browser mark

**Purpose:** Browser tabs, pinned shortcuts, and small web contexts.

Create: - A highly simplified version of the approved compact mark. -
Transparent and solid-background versions where technically useful. -
Test at 16 × 16 and 32 × 32 px. - Prefer a bold, unambiguous silhouette
over fine detail.

If the Arabic-derived symbol does not survive at favicon size, create a
dedicated simplified favicon derived from the same geometry and document
the simplification.

### Variant H --- Monochrome versions

Create exact one-color versions of every approved lockup:

-   Solid black
-   Solid white

Rules: - No gradients, shadows, glows, transparency effects, or
outlines. - Use the same paths and proportions as the master. - Confirm
that thin gaps do not close when printed or reduced.

### Variant I --- Brand-color versions

Use the established MASAR palette:

  Color        Hex         Role
  ------------ ----------- ----------------------------------
  Deep Green   `#0B3D2E`   Primary brand color
  Mint         `#14C88A`   Accent color
  White        `#FFFFFF`   Reversed mark and negative space
  Black        `#000000`   Monochrome mark
  Light Grey   `#F5F5F5`   Light neutral background

Recommended combinations: - White logo on deep green - Deep-green logo
on white - Black logo on white - White logo on black - Mint used as a
restrained accent, not as a replacement for every logo element

The primary logo should remain strong in a single color. Do not make
gradients mandatory for brand recognition.

### Variant J --- Small-size and low-detail version

**Purpose:** Tight UI spaces, small labels, and constrained placements.

Create a simplified version only if the master loses legibility at small
sizes.

-   Preserve the main silhouette.
-   Remove only details that are genuinely too fine to reproduce.
-   Do not change the Arabic spelling or letter structure.
-   Document the minimum recommended size for each lockup.

## 5. Color and background rules

### 5.1 Preferred backgrounds

1.  Black --- for the strongest monochrome presentation.
2.  Deep green `#0B3D2E` --- for the primary brand-color presentation.
3.  White --- for clean light interfaces and documents.
4.  Light grey `#F5F5F5` --- for subtle neutral applications.

### 5.2 Contrast

-   Use white artwork on dark backgrounds.
-   Use black or deep-green artwork on light backgrounds.
-   Keep the logo's contrast high.
-   Do not place the logo over busy photography unless a solid backing
    area preserves legibility.
-   Do not use mint as small text on white without checking contrast.

### 5.3 Effects

The standard logo is flat artwork. Optional motion or special campaign
treatments may use restrained mint light, but those effects must not
become part of the master logo.

Do not use: - Heavy neon glow - Metallic or chrome effects - 3D
extrusion - Drop shadows - Bevels - Uncontrolled gradients - Textures
that alter the letter edges

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Clear space and minimum size

Exact measurements must be established from the approved vector master.

### 6.1 Clear space

-   Define a repeatable clear-space unit from a feature of the Arabic
    mark.
-   Keep text, edges, images, and other logos outside this area.
-   Apply the same principle to every lockup.
-   Do not crop the mark or place it too close to a container edge.

### 6.2 Minimum size

Determine minimum sizes by testing actual exported artwork, not by
guessing.

Test: - Primary stacked lockup - Horizontal lockup - Arabic-only mark -
English-only wordmark - Compact mark - Favicon

At each size, confirm that the Arabic remains readable, counters remain
open, and the English line does not become noise. Record separate
minimum sizes for screen and print.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Typography for the English wordmark

The English wordmark must support the Arabic calligraphy rather than
compete with it.

Requirements: - Modern sans-serif appearance - Clean, simple
letterforms - Balanced spacing - Medium or regular visual weight,
depending on the final Arabic stroke weight - Consistent letter spacing
across all uses - No decorative serif, script, or futuristic display
font

The exact typeface is still a decision to confirm. Once selected, record
its family, weight, tracking, and whether the wordmark is live text or
converted to custom vector paths.

The Arabic logo itself should remain custom vector lettering and should
not be replaced by ordinary interface typography.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Composition rules

### Stacked lockup

-   Arabic centered above English.
-   English width should feel balanced with the Arabic mark.
-   Use optical centering.
-   Keep vertical spacing consistent.

### Horizontal lockup

-   Maintain Arabic dominance.
-   Use consistent gap and optical alignment.
-   Test both Arabic-first and English-first contexts.
-   Avoid placing English so close that the two elements merge.

### Compact mark

-   Center the approved derived symbol.
-   Preserve safe space on all sides.
-   Ensure the silhouette survives small-scale use.
-   Never add microtext.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Do and don't

### Do

-   Start from the approved master vector.
-   Preserve the exact Arabic lettering.
-   Use the same approved paths across variants.
-   Keep the identity minimal and premium.
-   Use black, white, and deep green as reliable primary treatments.
-   Use mint as a controlled accent.
-   Check every variant on real backgrounds and at real sizes.
-   Have Arabic lettering reviewed for correctness.

### Don't

-   Recreate the Arabic word from a generic font.
-   Change letter connections between variants.
-   Stretch or compress the logo.
-   Add a bus, road, pin, or arrow as an unrelated primary symbol.
-   Use the full bilingual logo when it becomes illegible.
-   Add glow or gradients to compensate for weak geometry.
-   Put tiny text inside the app icon.
-   Let AI-generated Arabic lettering become final production artwork
    without correction and verification.
-   Create different logo proportions for different platforms.

## 10. Production files and export package

Deliver editable master artwork and practical exports.

### 10.1 Editable source

-   SVG --- primary scalable vector source
-   AI or EPS --- editable professional vector source, if available
-   PDF --- vector-ready proof and print handoff

### 10.2 Raster exports

-   PNG with transparent background
-   PNG on black, white, and deep-green backgrounds
-   WebP where appropriate for web use
-   High-resolution PNG for presentations and previews

### 10.3 Required file naming

Use predictable names:

``` text
MASAR_Logo_Primary_Stacked_Dark.svg
MASAR_Logo_Primary_Stacked_Light.svg
MASAR_Logo_Arabic_Only.svg
MASAR_Logo_English_Only.svg
MASAR_Logo_Horizontal_Dark.svg
MASAR_Logo_Horizontal_Light.svg
MASAR_Logo_Compact_Mark.svg
MASAR_App_Icon_Master.svg
MASAR_Favicon.svg
MASAR_Logo_Black.svg
MASAR_Logo_White.svg
```

Add raster formats and size suffixes where needed, for example:

``` text
MASAR_App_Icon_1024.png
MASAR_Favicon_32.png
MASAR_Logo_Primary_Stacked_Dark_2x.png
```

### 10.4 Export quality checks

Before delivery: - Check transparent edges. - Check that no unintended
background is baked into transparent files. - Confirm all fonts are
outlined in final logo vectors or otherwise packaged and licensed
appropriately. - Confirm SVG paths render consistently across
browsers. - Check black and white versions at small sizes. - Confirm no
clipping, stray points, or accidental overlaps. - Open the exported
files in more than one viewer.

## 11. Generation brief for designers and image tools

Use this brief as the common instruction for every variant:

> Create a premium, minimalist logo variant for MASAR (مسار), based on
> the approved master Arabic-first calligraphic wordmark. Preserve the
> exact Arabic letterforms, spelling, connections, proportions, and
> silhouette from the supplied master artwork. The identity uses tall,
> elongated Arabic strokes, smooth controlled curves, refined negative
> space, and restrained modern English typography reading MASAR. The
> visual language is elegant, confident, and minimal. Use the approved
> black, white, deep green (#0B3D2E), and mint (#14C88A) palette. Do not
> invent new Arabic lettering, alter the spelling, add a generic
> transportation icon, or introduce unnecessary decorative effects.
> Present the requested variant clearly and separately on a plain
> background. Treat the supplied vector master as the source of truth.

### Variant-specific instruction templates

**Arabic-only** \> Use only the approved Arabic master wordmark مسار.
Remove the English support line without changing the Arabic artwork.
Center the Arabic mark with balanced clear space.

**English-only** \> Create the MASAR Latin wordmark using the approved
supporting type treatment. Keep it minimal, balanced, and visually
compatible with the Arabic master. Do not add a symbol.

**Horizontal** \> Arrange the approved Arabic master and MASAR English
wordmark in a clean horizontal lockup. Keep Arabic dominant, align
optically, and maintain a deliberate gap. Do not redraw either element.

**Compact app mark** \> Derive a compact app mark from the supplied
approved Arabic logo geometry. Simplify only where necessary for
small-size clarity. Do not invent a new symbol or alter the Arabic
meaning. Show the icon centered in a square safe area.

**Monochrome** \> Reproduce the supplied approved logo as a single flat
color: solid white on black, or solid black on white. Preserve all
original paths. No gradients, shadows, outlines, glow, or texture.

**Brand color** \> Reproduce the supplied approved logo with a flat
deep-green (#0B3D2E) treatment on white, or white on deep green. Mint
(#14C88A) may appear only as a small, optional accent. Preserve the
exact logo geometry.

**Favicon** \> Create a tiny-size favicon derived from the approved
compact mark. Prioritize a clear silhouette and strong contrast at 16 ×
16 and 32 × 32 pixels. Do not include the English wordmark or microtext.

**Important limitation:** Image-generation tools can help explore
compositions and visual directions, but they may distort Arabic
lettering. For final logo files, place the approved vector paths into
the layout manually rather than asking an image model to recreate the
wordmark.

## 12. Motion-ready logo preparation

Although this document focuses on static variants, prepare the master
artwork so it can later be animated.

-   Keep the Arabic mark as clean, separate vector paths where
    practical.
-   Preserve the natural drawing order of the Arabic strokes.
-   Keep the English letters as separate vector paths if they will be
    revealed sequentially.
-   Avoid unnecessary path complexity.
-   Retain a clean static master with no glow, blur, or motion effects.
-   Create a separate motion file; never replace the static master with
    an animated export.

The desired loading animation direction is a simple stroke-drawing
reveal: the logo is progressively drawn along its actual letter
contours, then the English MASAR wordmark appears. No particles, neon
waves, sweeping light beams, or unrelated visual effects.

## 13. Approval checklist

A variant is approved only when all applicable checks pass:

-   [ ] Correct MASAR identity and spelling
-   [ ] Arabic word reads exactly مسار
-   [ ] Arabic master geometry is unchanged
-   [ ] English wordmark is consistent
-   [ ] Correct lockup and alignment
-   [ ] Appropriate clear space
-   [ ] Strong contrast on intended background
-   [ ] Legible at intended size
-   [ ] No unapproved symbols or effects
-   [ ] SVG and PNG exports checked
-   [ ] Arabic review completed
-   [ ] Variant tested in its real application

## 14. Decisions still open

The following should be confirmed during final artwork production:

1.  Final vector geometry of the Arabic master wordmark.
2.  Final English wordmark typeface and tracking.
3.  Exact clear-space unit and minimum sizes.
4.  Whether the compact Arabic-derived mark is sufficiently
    recognizable.
5.  Final app icon and favicon simplification.
6.  Whether a horizontal lockup is needed in both RTL and LTR contexts.
7.  Final approved export dimensions for each platform.

Do not invent these measurements or treat exploratory mockups as
approved master artwork.

## 15. Master instruction

**All MASAR logo variants must be derived from one approved master.**

The identity should remain recognizable through its distinctive Arabic
calligraphy, refined proportions, restrained English typography, and
disciplined use of color. Adapt the layout to the application; never
alter the identity to fit the layout.
